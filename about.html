ABOUT THIS PROGRAM

🎯 Our Mission

The London Police Service is committed to public safety and maintaining the security of our community. The Wanted Persons Database is a tool designed to engage the public in assisting law enforcement by providing information about individuals wanted for criminal offences.

🤝 Community Partnership

We believe that effective policing happens through strong partnerships with our community. By sharing information about wanted persons, we empower our residents to play an active role in public safety efforts in London, Ontario.

🔒 Privacy & Anonymity

Your privacy is our priority. We protect the identities of all individuals who provide tips. You can report information anonymously through multiple channels, and we maintain strict confidentiality protocols.

📊 Database Information

This database is regularly updated with current wanted persons. Information is obtained from active investigations and may be classified by severity and status. Only individuals with active warrants or serious charges are listed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How often is this database updated?

A: The database is updated regularly as new warrants are issued and cases are resolved. Major updates occur daily.

Q: What should I do if I see a wanted person?

A: Contact local police immediately at 911 (emergency) or (519) 661-5670 (non-emergency). Do not attempt to apprehend them yourself.

Q: Can I report information anonymously?

A: Yes! All tip submission methods support anonymous reporting. You can contact Crime Stoppers at 1-800-222-TIPS (8477) for completely anonymous tips.

Q: Is there a reward for information?

A: Crime Stoppers may offer rewards for information leading to arrests. Contact Crime Stoppers directly for reward eligibility.

Q: How is my personal information protected?

A: All tip information is protected under privacy legislation. We do not share personal details of tipsters without consent, except as required by law.

Q: What if I have a tip about someone not on this list?

A: You can still report it! Contact (519) 661-5670 with any information about criminal activity or wanted persons.
